### 销售经验（可写入培训手册）
1. Never attempt to close before resolving the customer's stated objection; it damages trust and wastes rounds.
2. When a customer asks for specific policy details, do not repeat the same 'waiting' answer; proactively offer a concrete timeline and alternative proof (e.g., show a sample warranty document from another product).
3. In-store, use physical materials (brochures, printed policies) or call a colleague immediately to get answers, rather than leaving the customer waiting.
4. If you cannot provide information immediately, set a specific follow-up time and stick to it; avoid vague promises.
5. Use the customer's positive reaction to a demo as a bridge to address objections, not as a reason to skip them.

### 可复用策略模式
- **Policy-in-Hand Tactic**
  - 使用条件：When customer asks for warranty or policy details during an in-store interaction.
  - 示例：Have printed policy summaries or a tablet with policy PDFs ready. When asked, immediately hand over the document and highlight key points.
- **Demo-Bridge to Objection Resolution**
  - 使用条件：When customer has a positive demo experience but still has an unresolved objection.
  - 示例：Use the demo momentum to offer a related hands-on trial while you fetch the needed information, e.g., 'While I get the policy details, would you like to try the math app on the PaperMatte?'
- **Concrete Follow-Up Commitment**
  - 使用条件：When you cannot provide information immediately.
  - 示例：Set a specific time and channel: 'I will email you the full policy by 5 PM today. If it meets your needs, I will hold this unit for you at today's price until tomorrow.'
