### 销售经验（可写入培训手册）
1. When a customer repeatedly asks for a live demonstration, stop talking and perform the demo immediately. Do not substitute with explanations or post-purchase promises.
2. If you don't have a specific piece of data (e.g., brightness nits), use web_search to find it rather than claiming it's unpublished. Admitting lack of data without attempting to find it damages credibility.
3. For high-skepticism customers, every response should include a verifiable action (screen share, document, third-party link) within the first two rounds of the objection.
4. Avoid repeating the same argument (e.g., glare reduction) after the customer has explicitly rejected it. Shift to a different proof point or action.
5. In remote sales, always have a screen-sharing capability ready. If you cannot demonstrate live, schedule a follow-up call with the demo as the sole agenda.

### 可复用策略模式
- **Live Demo First Tactic**
  - 使用条件：When a customer asks for a demonstration of a specific feature (e.g., screen performance, app loading).
  - 示例：Customer: 'Show me the 4K timeline scrubbing.' Salesperson: 'Absolutely, I'm sharing my screen now. Watch as I scrub through this 4K timeline with effects — no lag.'
- **Spec Sheet Pull-Up Tactic**
  - 使用条件：When a customer asks for a specific specification (e.g., brightness nits, weight) that you don't have memorized.
  - 示例：Customer: 'What's the peak brightness?' Salesperson: 'Let me pull up the official spec sheet from Huawei's site. One moment... It says [X] nits. Here's the link for your reference.'
- **Action Over Words Tactic**
  - 使用条件：When a customer shows impatience or skepticism, especially after multiple explanations.
  - 示例：Customer: 'Enough talk.' Salesperson: 'You're right. Let me show you right now. I'm sharing my screen and performing the test.'
