### 销售经验（可写入培训手册）
1. Always complete the 3B (Resonate, Reframe, Evidence) on every objection before moving to close. Missing any step can lead to a failed gate condition.
2. When a customer repeats a request (e.g., 'zoom in closer'), it signals that the previous response was insufficient. Adapt by providing more granular evidence, not repeating the same line.
3. In-store demonstrations should include physical interaction: hand the device to the customer, let them adjust settings, and compare with competitors under the same lighting.
4. For legal professionals, emphasize pixel-level precision and outdoor readability. Use specific examples like punctuation marks in contracts.
5. Avoid premature closing. After the customer says 'I'm satisfied', reinforce the value and ask a confirmation question before closing.
