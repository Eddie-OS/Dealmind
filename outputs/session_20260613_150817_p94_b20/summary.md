### 失效操作
- 第1轮 [Using uncertain language ('I believe')]：The customer explicitly called out 'I believe' as insufficient ('thật không?'). This undermined trust and forced the customer to demand written confirmation, delaying the close.
  > Salesperson said: 'I believe we can push it closer to 10 triệu.'
- 第2轮 [Repeating the same reframe without new evidence]：The customer had already heard the reframe and wanted a concrete number. Repeating it wasted a turn and showed the salesperson was not listening to the customer's explicit request.
  > Salesperson repeated the cost-per-year reframe almost verbatim from round 1, without adding new information or addressing the customer's demand for a specific price.

### 销售经验（可写入培训手册）
1. Never use uncertain language like 'I believe' or 'approximately' when a customer demands a specific price; always provide a confirmed number or escalate to a manager immediately.
2. When a customer repeats a question, it signals that the previous answer was insufficient. Do not repeat the same reframe; instead, provide new information or a concrete action.
3. Use the customer's own test results as evidence to build credibility and reduce skepticism.
4. For price-sensitive customers, break down cost per year or per use to justify the investment.
5. Always have pre-approved discount figures and bundle details ready to avoid delays in closing.

### 可复用策略模式
- **Cost-Per-Year Reframe**
  - 使用条件：When customer objects to upfront price, especially for durable goods.
  - 示例：Instead of focusing on the price tag, calculate the cost per year of use. For a tablet expected to last 3 years, divide the price by 3 and compare to daily coffee expenses.
- **Customer Evidence Anchoring**
  - 使用条件：When customer has tested or used a similar product, or has personal experience.
  - 示例：Reference the customer's own test results: 'You just saw it run 3 apps smoothly — that's proof of performance. Now imagine that for the next 3 years.'
- **Manager Escalation for Certainty**
  - 使用条件：When customer demands a firm price or discount that the salesperson cannot confirm.
  - 示例：Immediately offer to check with a manager: 'Let me get my manager to confirm the exact price and discount. 60 seconds, and I'll have a written quote for you.'
