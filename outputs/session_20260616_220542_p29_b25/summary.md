### 失效操作
- 第6轮 [Closing prematurely without addressing availability]：The salesperson attempted to close before revealing that the product is not officially sold in Turkey, leading to a potential objection that was not handled earlier.
  > Salesperson said: 'Ready to lock one in today?'
- 第7轮 [Revealing unavailability after closing attempt]：This information should have been disclosed earlier to manage expectations. The salesperson risked losing the deal by surprising the customer with bad news after a positive interaction.
  > Salesperson said: 'MatePad Air isn't officially sold in Turkey yet, so I can't lock one locally.'

### 销售经验（可写入培训手册）
1. Always disclose product availability early to avoid surprising the customer after building excitement.
2. For technical customers, prioritize live demos and hands-on testing over verbal explanations.
3. Use specific technical metrics (e.g., 1.3mm key travel, sub-1ms latency) to build credibility with knowledgeable users.
4. When a product is not available locally, have a clear reservation or pre-order process ready to convert interest into a commitment.

### 更优话术模板
- **Product not available in Turkey**
  > I understand your concern. While the MatePad Air isn't officially launched in Turkey yet, I can offer you a priority reservation for the first batch. In the meantime, let me show you the full capabilities on this demo unit so you can be confident in your decision.
- **Price too high**
  > I see that price is a concern. Let me show you how the desktop mode and low-latency keyboard can replace a laptop, saving you from buying a separate device. Plus, with the included M-Pencil and WPS, you're getting a complete productivity suite.
- **Competitor has better specs**
  > I understand you're comparing specs. Let me set up a side-by-side demo with the competitor's device so you can see the real-world performance difference, especially in multitasking and keyboard latency.

### 可复用策略模式
- **Live Demo Immersion**
  - 使用条件：When the customer requests a feature demonstration, especially for technical products.
  - 示例：Customer asks to see VS Code in desktop mode. Salesperson immediately opens the app and lets the customer interact.
- **Hands-On Validation**
  - 使用条件：When the customer is skeptical about performance claims.
  - 示例：Customer wants to test key travel. Salesperson invites them to type and run a script to feel the latency.
- **Early Availability Disclosure**
  - 使用条件：When the product is not yet launched in the customer's market.
  - 示例：At the start of the interaction, say: 'This model is coming soon to Turkey. Let me show you what it can do, and I can reserve one for you.'
