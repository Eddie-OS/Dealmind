### 有效策略
- 第4轮 [Apologize and comply with customer's request immediately]：It broke the cycle of redundant confirmation and directly addressed the customer's core demand for a hands-on test, restoring trust and moving the conversation forward.
  > 销冠: 'You're right — I've been repeating myself, and I apologize. Let me cut straight to what you've asked for... Here — I'm opening a fresh 20+ layer file from the gallery right now.'
- 第5轮 [Hands-off demonstration (let customer test themselves)]：Empowered the customer to prove the product's capability herself, which is the most convincing form of evidence for a skeptical buyer. The customer immediately confirmed no lag and was impressed.
  > 销冠: 'Here's the fresh 20+ layer file. Draw fast, use big brushes, then flatten everything yourself. Feel the performance with your own hands — no questions, just your test. Go ahead.'

### 失效操作
- 第1轮 [Repeated confirmation despite customer's explicit request]：The customer had already stated her concern clearly and asked for a test. Repeating the confirmation wasted time and frustrated the customer, who perceived it as stalling.
  > 销冠: 'So to confirm: your main concern is that the nano-etching is permanent and won't wear off, and that the tablet stays cool and doesn't lag when merging 20+ layers — correct? Once you say yes, I'll open a fresh gallery file...'
- 第2轮 [Ignoring customer's direct instruction and repeating confirmation again]：The customer had already said 'nano-etching and heat are fine' in the previous round. Repeating the same confirmation showed poor listening and eroded trust, leading to an angry response.
  > 销冠: 'So just to confirm — your main concern is that the nano-etching is permanent and won't wear off, and that the tablet stays cool and doesn't lag when merging 20+ layers — correct? Once you say yes, I'll open a fresh gallery file...'

### 销售经验（可写入培训手册）
1. When a customer explicitly asks for a hands-on test, do not stall with repeated confirmations. Apologize and comply immediately.
2. For skeptical buyers, the most effective proof is letting them test the product themselves. Verbal claims are insufficient.
3. Listen to the customer's words: if they say 'I already told you' and 'stop asking', it's a red flag that you are wasting their time. Pivot immediately.
4. In a retail setting, always have a demo file ready for stress tests. Delaying the demo can cost the sale.
5. Use the customer's own testing as a closing tool: after they confirm no lag, ask a closing question like 'So would you like to take this home today?'

### 可复用策略模式
- **Immediate Compliance Tactic**
  - 使用条件：When a customer explicitly asks for a specific action (e.g., test, demo, price match) and shows signs of impatience.
  - 示例：Customer: 'Just open the file and let me test.' Salesperson: 'Right away. Here it is. Please test it yourself.'
- **Hands-On Proof Close**
  - 使用条件：When a customer is skeptical about performance claims and wants to verify personally.
  - 示例：After the customer tests and confirms no lag, say: 'You've proven it yourself. So, would you like to take this model home today?'
- **Apology Pivot**
  - 使用条件：When the salesperson realizes they have been repeating themselves or ignoring the customer's request.
  - 示例：Salesperson: 'You're right, I apologize. Let me stop and do exactly what you asked.' Then immediately comply.
