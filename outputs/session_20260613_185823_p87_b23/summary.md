### 销售经验（可写入培训手册）
1. Always provide the price in the customer's local currency (PLN) immediately when asked, especially for price-sensitive prospects.
2. When facing a competitor price gap, break down the difference into specific, quantifiable benefits (e.g., savings on subscriptions, hardware advantages).
3. If a live demo is not possible, use screen sharing, pre-recorded videos, or detailed specifications to simulate the experience and build trust.
4. Use a challenge-based close ('test it yourself, if it fails walk away') to reduce risk perception and accelerate decisions.
5. For high-skepticism customers, avoid vague claims; provide permanent vs. trial, latency numbers, and refresh rates as concrete evidence.

### 可复用策略模式
- **Price Gap Decomposition**
  - 使用条件：When customer compares your price to a cheaper competitor and asks 'what do I get for the extra?'
  - 示例：Break the difference into 2-3 specific, monetizable benefits (e.g., 'That 150 zł buys you permanent WPS worth 120 zł/year, a matte screen that reduces eye strain, and a faster keyboard').
- **Challenge-Based Close**
  - 使用条件：When customer requests a demo or test before buying, and you are confident in the product's performance.
  - 示例：Say: 'Test it yourself. If it doesn't meet your expectations, walk away. If it does, we have a deal at [price]. Fair?' This reduces perceived risk and builds trust.
- **Immediate Local Price Disclosure**
  - 使用条件：When customer asks for price in their local currency, especially if they are price-sensitive.
  - 示例：Always respond with the exact price in the customer's currency (e.g., '1,999 zł') in the first reply, without delay or qualification.
