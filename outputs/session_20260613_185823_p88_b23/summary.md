### 销售经验（可写入培训手册）
1. Always start with empathy: acknowledge the customer's pain point and its impact before moving to solutions. For example, 'I understand how frustrating it must have been to lose a meeting due to battery failure. Let me show you how MatePad Air addresses that.'
2. Avoid over-confirming the same requirements. Once you have a clear understanding, propose a next step or value proposition to advance the conversation.
3. When a customer asks for evidence, provide it promptly and specifically. Use concrete numbers and demonstrations, not summaries.
4. In follow-up scenarios, set a clear timeline and deliverable list to manage expectations and build trust.
5. If the customer is skeptical, use third-party data or demonstrations to build credibility. However, ensure all claims are verifiable.

### 可复用策略模式
- **Evidence-Based Confirmation Loop**
  - 使用条件：When customer demands proof before commitment.
  - 示例：Customer: 'I need to see battery numbers.' Salesperson: 'I'll send you the raw drain percentages from a 2-hour stress test. If they meet your expectations, we can discuss next steps. Fair?'
- **Empathy-First Acknowledgment**
  - 使用条件：When customer shares a negative experience or pain point.
  - 示例：Customer: 'My tablet died during a presentation.' Salesperson: 'That must have been embarrassing and costly. I'm sorry that happened. Let me show you how our tablet prevents that.'
- **Live Demo Over Email**
  - 使用条件：When customer requests visual proof and you are in a physical store or have a demo unit.
  - 示例：Customer: 'I need to see WPS formatting.' Salesperson: 'Let me open a .key file on this unit right now. You can see the tables and fonts are preserved. Would you like to try it yourself?'
