### 销售经验（可写入培训手册）
1. Always have Care+ pricing and warranty details ready before the conversation to avoid delays.
2. When a customer asks about a bundle discount, either provide a definitive answer or offer a small concession (e.g., free accessory) instead of repeating the same total.
3. In in-store scenarios, use physical demonstration to build trust—show the screen durability or let the customer hold the device.
4. Use the 'cost avoidance' framing: compare Care+ cost to potential repair costs to justify the investment.
5. For skeptical customers, provide exact numbers and written confirmation to reduce anxiety.
