### 销售经验（可写入培训手册）
1. Always use the customer's language consistently; avoid mixing languages mid-sentence.
2. When official pricing is unavailable, provide a reasonable estimate and offer to notify the customer upon launch to maintain trust.
3. Leverage certifications (e.g., SGS) to substantiate claims, especially for health-related features like eye comfort.
4. Summarize demonstrated benefits before attempting a close to reinforce value.
5. For high-skepticism customers, use multiple demonstrations (e-book, dark scene, tilt) to address specific concerns.

### 更优话术模板
- **Price unknown for Italy**

- **Warranty and service location**
  > Absolutely, the 2-year EU warranty is standard. And once launched, Huawei has an official service center in Milan—no shipping needed. You'll have full local support.
- **Screen quality for family use**
  > Let me show you. Here's a dark scene with subtitles—see how the blacks stay deep even from the side? And with PaperMatte, no glare. Your whole family will enjoy movie nights without eye strain.

### 可复用策略模式
- **Certification Anchoring**
  - 使用条件：When customer questions health/quality claims (e.g., eye comfort, safety).
  - 示例：This eye comfort mode is SGS-certified by a Swiss institute, not just a brand claim—proven to reduce eye strain.
- **Transparent Pre-Launch Commitment**
  - 使用条件：When product is not yet available in the customer's market.
  - 示例：I can't give a confirmed price yet, but I estimate €380–420. Let me note your details and send you the official price and launch date the moment they're confirmed.
- **Benefit Recap Close**
  - 使用条件：After multiple demonstrations, before asking for commitment.
  - 示例：You've seen the anti-glare, the 144Hz motion, the eye comfort, and the dark scene contrast. Shall we get this wrapped up for your family?
