### 销售经验（可写入培训手册）
1. Always connect price discussion back to demonstrated value using a spin_need_payoff question to prevent price-only focus.
2. When customer mentions a competitor device (e.g., Mi Pad 5 Pro), immediately probe for pain points to strengthen your product's differentiation.
3. Use specific local details (exact store, address, warranty center name) to build trust and remove last objections.
4. In in-store demos, invite physical handling (hold the device, compare weight) to engage more senses and increase ownership feeling.
5. After confirming warranty and price, use an assumptive close with a specific next step (e.g., 'Which installment tenure?') to move to closing.

### 更优话术模板
- **Customer asks about price without seeing value**
  > I understand price is important. Before we talk numbers, let me show you how the [product] handles [specific task you care about]—I think you'll be impressed. [Proceed with live demo].
- **Customer compares with competitor's cheaper model**
  > I see you're considering [competitor model]. Let's do a quick side-by-side test here—open the same app on both devices and see which one loads faster and stays cooler. [Invite customer to hold both].
- **Customer hesitates on warranty coverage**
  > Here's the official warranty card—it covers parts and labor for one year at any authorized center. Let me show you the list of centers in [city]; the one at [specific address] is just [distance] from here. [Point to physical map or show on phone].

### 可复用策略模式
- **Live Demo Value Anchoring**
  - 使用条件：When customer shows interest but asks about price or warranty before seeing full value.
  - 示例：After customer asks price, say: 'Let me first show you how it handles [their use case]—then we can talk about the investment.' Then run a quick demo and ask: 'How would that performance change your daily workflow?'
- **Local Specificity Trust Builder**
  - 使用条件：When customer asks about warranty or service locations.
  - 示例：Instead of saying 'we have centers in your city', say: 'The authorized center at [exact street name, district] is just [distance] from here. I can show you on the map.' This leverages local knowledge to build trust.
- **Assumptive Close with Installment Options**
  - 使用条件：After all objections are resolved and customer shows buying signals.
  - 示例：Say: 'Great choice! Let me confirm your order. Which installment plan works best for you—6, 9, or 12 months?' This assumes the sale and gives a limited choice to close.
