### 销售经验（可写入培训手册）
1. Always provide a specific price when asked, even if it's an estimate, and commit to confirming the official price within a short timeframe.
2. Never repeat the same stalling response more than once; if you don't have the answer, acknowledge the delay and give a concrete timeline.
3. Use web search tools proactively to fetch official pricing from the customer's local store before the conversation if possible.
4. When a customer reveals their budget, immediately confirm whether your product fits and offer a reservation or hold to secure the deal.
5. Avoid deflecting to competitor comparisons when the customer is asking for a direct price; address the price question first.
