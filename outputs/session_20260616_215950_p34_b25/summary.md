### 销售经验（可写入培训手册）
1. Always have the exact final price ready for the most common discount scenario. If you can't calculate it immediately, use a tool or ask a colleague – never give ranges when the customer asks for a single number.
2. In an in-store setting, never send the customer to an online platform for pricing. Use the store's own price list or POS system to quote the price. If you must reference online, do it discreetly and immediately translate to in-store terms.
3. When a customer asks the same question three times, you are failing to listen. Pivot to a direct answer without additional conditions. The first time they ask for a final number, give it.
4. Honesty about limitations (e.g., 'I can't confirm stacking') builds trust, but follow it up with a clear best-case scenario that the customer can act on immediately.
5. Avoid mixing multiple discount paths unless the customer explicitly asks. Focus on the most straightforward offer first, then upsell later.

### 更优话术模板
- **I need a final number, not ranges.**
  > Absolutely. Here is the exact price you pay today with the [coupon name]: [exact amount] [currency]. No trade-in, no conditions. That's the total out the door. Does that work for you?
- **Does this coupon stack with trade-in?**
  > I checked our system: the coupon and trade-in are separate promotions. I can apply one or the other. Let me show you the best option right now on this screen. [Point to POS or device] For your business, the coupon gives you [exact amount] off immediately. Would you like to go with that?
- **I want to compare this with online prices.**
  > I understand. Let me pull up our in-store price on this tablet right here – it's the same as online, but you get it today with no shipping wait. Plus, you can try the keyboard and M-Pencil right now. [Hand device to customer] See how the glare-free screen looks under these lights? That's something you can't test online.

### 可复用策略模式
- **Exact Number First**
  - 使用条件：When a customer asks for a price or final cost, especially after expressing frustration with ranges.
  - 示例：Customer: 'Give me the final price.' Salesperson: 'With the [offer], your total today is [exact amount]. No conditions.'
- **Honest Limitation + Best Path**
  - 使用条件：When you cannot confirm a discount stacking or policy detail.
  - 示例：Customer: 'Does this stack with trade-in?' Salesperson: 'I can't confirm stacking, but here's the best single offer: [exact amount] off. That's the number you can lock right now.'
- **In-Store Demo Redirect**
  - 使用条件：When a customer compares online or asks for specs that are better experienced physically.
  - 示例：Customer: 'Is the screen really glare-free?' Salesperson: 'Let me show you. [Turn on device under bright light] See how clear it is? You can't test that online. Here, try typing on the keyboard.'
