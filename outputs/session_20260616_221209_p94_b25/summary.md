### 销售经验（可写入培训手册）
1. When a customer repeatedly asks for a guarantee, stop deflecting and offer a concrete, written guarantee immediately. Delaying erodes trust.
2. In in-store sales, always use physical demonstrations (e.g., show the app working on the device) to build trust, not just verbal promises.
3. Avoid repeating the same paraphrasing technique if the customer rejects it; pivot to a solution-oriented response.
4. For skeptical customers, acknowledge their concern directly and provide a risk-reversal mechanism (e.g., money-back guarantee) early in the conversation.
5. Ensure all offers (e.g., guarantee) are documented in writing to match the customer's expectation of a 'written guarantee'.

### 更优话术模板
- **Can you offer a 30-day money-back guarantee if VPBank NEO fails?**

- **Xiaomi Pad has native Google Play, so why should I trust MatePad?**
  > I understand the concern. But let's test it together. Here's a demo MatePad Air with VPBank NEO installed. You can log in and try a transaction right now. If it works smoothly, you'll see the experience is identical. And with our written guarantee, you have zero risk.
- **GBox is a patch, not a promise. What if it breaks mid-semester?**
  > That's a valid worry. That's why we offer a 30-day written guarantee. But also, let me show you how GBox works on this device. It's pre-installed and optimized. You can test it with your banking app right now. If there's any issue, you're covered.

### 可复用策略模式
- **Risk-Reversal Guarantee**
  - 使用条件：When a customer expresses fear of product failure or incompatibility, especially with banking apps.
  - 示例：Offer a written 30-day money-back guarantee if the app fails, and show the guarantee document immediately.
- **In-Store Live Demo**
  - 使用条件：When a customer doubts app compatibility or performance.
  - 示例：Invite the customer to test the app on a demo unit right there, saying 'Let's try it now and see for yourself.'
- **Direct Acknowledgment + Solution**
  - 使用条件：When a customer repeats the same objection more than twice.
  - 示例：Say 'You're right, I haven't given you a straight answer. Here it is: [direct solution].' Then provide the solution.
