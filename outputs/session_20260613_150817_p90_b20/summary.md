### 可复用策略模式
- **Live A/B Comparison**
  - 使用条件：When the user compares with a competitor product and is in-store.
  - 示例：Set up two tablets side-by-side, play the same content, and let the user interact. Ask: 'Which one sounds clearer to you when you pan?'

  - 使用条件：When the user shows interest but hesitates on price or commitment.
