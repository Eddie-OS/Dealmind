### 销售经验（可写入培训手册）
1. Always start with a warm greeting and space-giving to reduce initial tension, especially with skeptical buyers.
2. Use live demonstrations (spotlight, window, flex test) to provide tangible proof for technical objections.
3. After addressing objections, spend time anchoring value (S3) before closing to reinforce the product's fit.
4. For skeptical buyers, invite them to test the product themselves rather than relying on verbal claims.
5. In-store demos should be multi-sensory (visual, tactile) to build trust quickly.

- **Is the keyboard as good as the Magic Keyboard?**

- **Real-World Environment Test**
  - 使用条件：When customer questions performance in specific conditions (e.g., outdoor).
  - 示例：Move to a window or outside to test screen visibility in real daylight.
- **Physical Durability Challenge**
  - 使用条件：When customer questions build quality or durability.
  - 示例：Invite customer to flex or twist the device to prove sturdiness.
