### 销售经验（可写入培训手册）
1. 1. Never repeat the same response without new action; it frustrates the customer and wastes rounds.
2. 2. If you cannot answer a technical spec, immediately escalate to a reliable source (e.g., manufacturer support) rather than promising a call that may not happen.
3. 3. When promising a call or action, ensure it is executed within the conversation or provide a clear timeline.
4. 4. Use honesty as a trust-building tool: admitting limitations and offering a concrete next step can salvage the interaction.
5. 5. For pragmatic customers, focus on delivering proof (e.g., specs, user testimonials) rather than emotional reassurance.
