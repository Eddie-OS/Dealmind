### 有效策略
- 第2轮 [Direct answer after apology]：Customer had expressed frustration with indirect answers; directness rebuilt trust and moved conversation forward.
  > Salesperson said: 'Habibti, forgive me — let me be direct. The MatePad Air's 10,100mAh battery is rated for a full day...'
- 第6轮 [SPIN implication question]：Deepened the pain point beyond battery to emotional/academic impact, increasing urgency and value of solution.
  > Salesperson asked: 'When your daughter misses key explanations because the old tablet dies, does it shake her confidence in class or affect her grades?'

### 失效操作
- 第1轮 [SPIN implication question too early]：Customer had already stated the problem twice; repeating it frustrated her and delayed direct answer.
  > Salesperson asked: '...does she miss important explanations, or does she scramble to reconnect and lose focus?'
- 第6轮 [Repeating implication question after customer objected]：Ignored customer's explicit request to move forward, risking trust and prolonging negotiation.
  > Salesperson asked again about grades after customer said 'let's not go backwards'.

### 销售经验（可写入培训手册）
1. Always listen for repeated questions and answer directly the first time to avoid frustration.
2. Use SPIN implication questions only after the customer has fully expressed the problem; avoid re-asking.
3. In-store: always offer a hands-on demo (e.g., show fast charging speed) to build trust.
4. When customer says 'let's not go backwards', immediately pivot to closing or value anchoring.
5. For price-sensitive but low-bargaining customers, state the final price clearly and offer a reservation to create urgency.

### 更优话术模板
- **Customer asks repeatedly about battery life after initial answer.**
  > I understand you want certainty. Let me show you right here on the device — we can run a video call and see the battery drain in real time. If it doesn't meet 6 hours, I'll personally help you find another option.
- **Customer asks for extra discount after bundle promotion.**
  > Habibti, this bundle already includes the M-Pencil and keyboard cover at a Ramadan price. I can't reduce further, but I can add a free screen protector and case from our stock today. Shall I prepare the package?
- **Customer questions warranty coverage.**
  > Let me show you our warranty card — it lists all local service centers in Doha. You can also scan this QR code to see the nearest one. No need to ship abroad.

### 可复用策略模式

- **Implication Deep Dive**

  - 示例：'When [pain point] happens, does it affect [emotional/academic outcome]?'
- **Reservation Close**
  - 使用条件：When customer agrees on price and warranty but hesitates.
