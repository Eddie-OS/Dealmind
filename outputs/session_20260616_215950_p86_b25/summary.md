### 销售经验（可写入培训手册）
1. When a customer demands proof of performance, immediately offer a live in-store demonstration of their exact use case (e.g., 'Let's open 20 tabs and your Excel file right now').
2. Use a risk-reversal guarantee (e.g., 30-day return, no restocking fee) to overcome skepticism, especially for high-involvement tech purchases.
3. Avoid dismissing the customer's objection by suggesting a workaround (e.g., 'keep your MacBook for heavy tasks') — it undermines the product's value proposition.
4. Once the customer agrees to a trial, move to closing (e.g., 'Let me print the guarantee and you can take it home today') instead of repeating the same guarantee.
5. In a retail setting, always have a demo unit ready to run customer-specific tests — this builds trust faster than any spec sheet.

### 可复用策略模式
- **Live Stress Test Challenge**
  - 使用条件：When a customer questions product performance under heavy load, especially for power users.
  - 示例：Customer: 'Can it handle 20 tabs and Excel?' Salesperson: 'Let's test it right now. I'll open 20 tabs and a large Excel file. If it stutters, you walk. If it holds, you take the trial. Deal?'
- **Written Guarantee Close**
  - 使用条件：When a customer needs risk elimination before committing, especially after a successful demo.
  - 示例：Customer: 'I'll take the trial but I want it in writing.' Salesperson: 'Absolutely. I'm printing the guarantee now: 30-day return, no restocking fee. Sign here and take it home today.'
- **Challenge-Response Commitment**
  - 使用条件：When a customer sets a condition for purchase (e.g., 'if it passes my test, I'll buy').
  - 示例：Customer: 'If it doesn't stutter, I'll take it.' Salesperson: 'Deal. Watch closely — 20 tabs loading now... Still smooth. Here's your trial unit.'
