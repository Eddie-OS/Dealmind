### 销售经验（可写入培训手册）
1. Always schedule a specific follow-up time when a customer needs to consult others; don't leave it open-ended.
2. After addressing an objection, actively check for remaining concerns using a question like 'Does that fully address your worry?' to ensure complete resolution.
3. In-store demos should be interactive and tied to the customer's specific use case (e.g., law students tagging PDFs).
4. When a customer mentions needing approval, offer to provide a printed summary or a demo for the decision-maker to build trust.
