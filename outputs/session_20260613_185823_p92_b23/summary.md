### 销售经验（可写入培训手册）
1. Always deliver promised evidence (video, demo) within the same conversation; never leave it as a follow-up if the customer is ready to decide.
2. Prepare key information (e.g., student discounts, bundle pricing) in advance so you can answer immediately when the customer asks.
3. Use assumptive closes (e.g., color choice) after addressing objections to test commitment and move the sale forward.
4. When a customer explicitly states conditions for purchase, confirm them and then systematically fulfill each one before asking for the order.
5. If you cannot provide an answer immediately, set a specific, short timeframe (e.g., 'within 30 minutes') and get the customer's email to follow up, but try to avoid this if possible.

### 可复用策略模式
- **Conditional Commitment Loop**
  - 使用条件：When a customer states specific conditions for purchase (e.g., 'I need to see X before I buy').
  - 示例：Customer: 'I need to see the video first.' Salesperson: 'I’ll show you the video now. After you see it, if it meets your expectations, are you ready to proceed? Great, here it is.'
- **Assumptive Close with Options**
  - 使用条件：After addressing the main objection, immediately offer a choice between two positive options.
  - 示例：Customer: 'The battery sounds good.' Salesperson: 'So, would you prefer the Graphite Black or the White model? Both come with the full bundle.'
- **Pre-emptive Price Preparation**
  - 使用条件：Before the customer asks for pricing, have all bundle and discount figures ready.
  - 示例：Salesperson: 'The full bundle including student discount is £404.99. I have the exact figure ready for you.'
