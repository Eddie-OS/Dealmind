### 销售经验（可写入培训手册）
1. Always prepare estimated local pricing before the interaction, especially for pre-launch products. Use exchange rate and regional pricing to give a ballpark figure.
2. For warranty-sensitive customers, have a clear, documented answer on coverage for normal wear vs. defects. If unsure, escalate immediately rather than promising a callback.
3. When a customer states a dealbreaker, address it head-on with a definitive answer or a concrete action (e.g., call service team on the spot) instead of deferring.
4. In-store, leverage physical demo to overcome objections: e.g., show scratch resistance by demonstrating with a stylus, or run a heat test.
5. Use price protection as a closing tool: offer to lock the lower estimated price to reduce risk and encourage commitment.

### 可复用策略模式
- **Hands-on trust builder**
  - 使用条件：When customer doubts product feel or performance, especially for creative tools.
  - 示例：Invite them to draw, type, or test the feature immediately. 'Here, try it yourself — the screen has a subtle drag, not glassy.'
- **Price protection close**
  - 使用条件：When official pricing is not yet announced but customer needs a number to decide.
  - 示例：Offer an estimated price with a guarantee to match the lower official price if it launches cheaper. 'I'll reserve at ฿18,000 with price protection.'
- **Dealbreaker triage**
  - 使用条件：When customer states a non-negotiable condition (e.g., warranty, specific feature).
  - 示例：Immediately address it with a concrete action: call the service team, show a demo, or provide a written policy excerpt. Do not defer without a timeline.
