- 第4轮 [Lack of objection handling for sound quality]：Giulia's skepticism (0.85) and competitor awareness (0.9) meant she might have considered other brands; not addressing potential sound limitations could have lost the deal if she had a stronger objection.

### 销售经验（可写入培训手册）
1. 1. When a customer sets a hard price anchor (e.g., 'under €480'), confirm the exact price early and link it to the bundle to remove price objection.
2. 2. For family buyers, always demonstrate both the primary use case (movie night sound) and secondary use case (homework keyboard) with hands-on testing.
3. 3. Avoid repetitive closing; instead, add new value or address unspoken concerns each time you ask for the sale.
4. 4. If a customer repeatedly emphasizes a feature (e.g., 'sound must fill the room'), proactively offer a comparison or upgrade option to preempt objections.
5. 5. In-store, leverage multi-sensory demonstrations (touch, sound, sight) to build trust and reduce skepticism.

### 可复用策略模式
- **Price Anchor Confirmation**
  - 使用条件：Customer states a specific budget or price limit early in the conversation.
  - 示例：When Giulia said 'under €480', the salesperson immediately confirmed '€480 net' and linked it to the bundle, removing price as a barrier.
- **Multi-Sensory Demo Loop**
  - 使用条件：Customer has two distinct use cases (e.g., movie night + homework) and wants to test both.
  - 示例：Salesperson let Giulia type on the keyboard (touch) and listen to speakers (sound) in sequence, then tied both to the family benefit.
- **Benefit Stacking Close**
  - 使用条件：After multiple demonstrations, customer shows positive signals but hasn't committed.
  - 示例：Salesperson summarized all benefits (keyboard, sound, screen, price) in one sentence before asking for the close: '€480 net: tablet, Folio Keyboard, €30 credit. Shall I ring it up for your family?'
