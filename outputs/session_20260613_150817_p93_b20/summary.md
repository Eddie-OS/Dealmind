### 销售经验（可写入培训手册）
1. Always recap and confirm customer pain points early to build trust, especially with skeptical buyers.
2. When a product has a weakness (e.g., keyboard flex on lap), proactively offer a physical accessory or workaround to turn it into a solution.
3. Avoid making unsubstantiated competitor claims; instead, let the customer experience the difference firsthand.
4. In a retail setting, use every opportunity for hands-on demonstration to overcome objections.
5. For proxy buyers (e.g., child buying for parent), address the end-user's concerns directly and provide clear, verifiable answers.

### 可复用策略模式
- **Pain Point Recap Trust Builder**
  - 使用条件：When customer has multiple pain points and high skepticism.
  - 示例：After initial needs discovery, summarize the customer's pain points and ask for confirmation before proposing solutions.
- **Accessory Solution Turnaround**
  - 使用条件：When a product feature has a known weakness in a specific use case.
  - 示例：If the keyboard wobbles on lap, immediately offer a lap desk or rigid surface and let the customer test it.
- **Live Demo Competitor Deflection**
  - 使用条件：When customer mentions a competitor and you have a comparative advantage.
  - 示例：Instead of making claims, set up a side-by-side test and let the customer experience the difference.
