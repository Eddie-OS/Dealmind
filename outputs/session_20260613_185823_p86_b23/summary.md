### 销售经验（可写入培训手册）
1. When a customer demands written terms, send them immediately or provide a live link; do not promise to send 'within the hour' if the customer expects instant delivery.
2. For battery objections, always propose specific, adjustable settings (e.g., brightness, power-saving mode) and project the new runtime to create a tangible benchmark.
3. If the official website is down, use alternative trusted sources (e.g., Amazon UK, Currys) to verify claims, or acknowledge the limitation and offer a written guarantee with a correction clause.
4. Avoid repeating the same promise; if the customer pushes for faster action, escalate the response (e.g., send the email on the spot).
5. For skeptical buyers, always back claims with written or third-party evidence; verbal 'typical' statements erode trust.
