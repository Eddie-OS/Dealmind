### 有效策略
- 第3轮 [Spec confirmation with evidence]：Directly addressed the customer's explicit requirement (400 nits) with a precise, verified number (500 nits), building credibility and overcoming the brightness objection.
  > Salesperson: 'I just checked the official spec — the MatePad Air's display is rated at 500 nits peak brightness, well above your 400-nit minimum.'
- 第5轮 [Benefit stacking with specific numbers]：Provided concrete battery capacity and charging speed, directly matching the customer's stated need for 10 hours of use, and added a compelling fast-charging benefit.
  > Salesperson: '10,100mAh battery — that's a full day of note-taking and streaming without reaching for a charger. And if you do run low, 66W fast charging gives you hours of use from just a 30-minute top-up.'

### 失效操作
- 第2轮 [Vague response without immediate answer]：The salesperson failed to have the spec ready, causing a delay and forcing the customer to repeat the request. This could have eroded trust if not corrected quickly.
  > Salesperson: 'I couldn't pull the exact nit number just now — let me confirm it for you from the official spec sheet.'
- 第4轮 [Missing proactive objection handling]：The salesperson jumped to answer without exploring the customer's specific use case, missing an opportunity to tailor the response and build deeper rapport.
  > Customer: 'How's the battery life? I need at least 10 hours...' The salesperson did not ask about usage patterns or confirm the need before answering.

### 销售经验（可写入培训手册）
1. Always have key specs (brightness, battery, charging) ready to answer immediately; delays can undermine trust.
2. When a customer states a minimum requirement, confirm it with a specific number that exceeds it to build confidence.
3. Proactively ask about usage patterns before answering feature questions to tailor the response and uncover additional needs.
4. Use benefit stacking with concrete numbers (e.g., '10,100mAh gives you a full day') to make features tangible.

### 可复用策略模式
- **Spec Overdelivery**
  - 使用条件：When customer states a minimum requirement (e.g., brightness, battery life).
  - 示例：Customer: 'I need at least 400 nits.' Salesperson: 'This model has 500 nits peak brightness, well above your requirement.'
- **Benefit Stacking with Numbers**
  - 使用条件：When presenting a feature that has multiple measurable benefits.
  - 示例：Customer asks about battery. Salesperson: 'It has a 10,100mAh battery for all-day use, and 66W fast charging gives you hours of use from a 30-minute charge.'
- **Objection Preemption**
  - 使用条件：When a common objection is likely (e.g., software flexibility for tech-savvy users).
  - 示例：Salesperson: 'I know you might be concerned about sideloading apps. HarmonyOS allows APK installation, and for Google apps, GBox works seamlessly.'
