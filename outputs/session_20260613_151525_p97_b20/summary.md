2. Secure a specific follow-up time: When a customer says 'déjame pensarlo', immediately set a concrete appointment (e.g., 'I'll call you at 10 AM tomorrow') and offer a time-limited incentive to encourage return.
3. Leverage the husband's involvement: Since Rosa has low decision power, invite her husband to the store for a joint demo or offer a 'spouse discount' to close the deal faster.
4. Use scarcity effectively: In round 9, the salesperson started to say 'hold this unit and the bundle price for...' but didn't specify a deadline. Always state a clear expiration (e.g., 'until tomorrow 6 PM').
5. Avoid mentioning tool downtime: Instead of saying the tool is down, say 'Let me check the latest pricing for you right now' and use a printed list or manual calculation to maintain confidence.
