### 销售经验（可写入培训手册）
1. When a customer mentions needing to consult a spouse, immediately offer a joint demo or a take-home trial to reduce the need for a second visit.
2. For return policy objections, reframe the warranty as a 'risk-free trial' by emphasizing defect coverage and offering a satisfaction guarantee if possible.
3. Use scarcity tactics (e.g., limited-time bundle, stock availability) to create urgency, especially for price-sensitive customers who are on the fence.
4. In-store, always invite the customer to physically interact with the product (e.g., let them write with the stylus, open a PDF) to build emotional connection.
5. Prepare a 'spouse objection' rebuttal script that includes a specific time-limited offer to incentivize immediate decision.

### 可复用策略模式
- **Live Demo Anchoring**
  - 使用条件：When customer doubts product quality or screen performance.
  - 示例：Let the customer physically use the tablet to read a PDF or draw, then compare with a competitor device on the spot.
- **Value Stacking Comparison**
  - 使用条件：When customer compares price with a competitor.
  - 示例：List the included accessories and their retail prices to show total savings, e.g., 'Same price as Xiaomi, but you get S/300 in free accessories.'

  - 使用条件：When customer says they need to consult a partner.
