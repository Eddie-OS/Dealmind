### 有效策略
- 第4轮 [现场演示破信任法]：Directly addressed the user's skepticism about Google Play compatibility by letting them try it themselves, which built trust and moved the conversation forward.
  > Salesperson said: 'Here's GBox on the demo unit — tap it, sign in with your Google account, and you'll see Play Store, Gmail, YouTube all running natively.'
- 第7轮 [价格锚定与替代方案]：Acknowledged the budget objection and offered a lower-priced variant that still met the user's key requirements, making the deal possible.
  > Salesperson said: 'The good news: the 8GB+256GB standard model is 48,999 ₽ at M.Video right now, within your 50–55k budget. Same 10,000mAh battery and 144Hz screen, just less RAM.'

### 失效操作
- 第6轮 [初始报价过高]：The upper end of the price range (55,990 ₽) exceeded the user's budget of 50-55k ₽, triggering a strong objection. The salesperson should have immediately offered the lower-priced model.
  > Salesperson said: 'the MatePad 12 X is priced from 34,499 ₽ to 55,990 ₽ at DNS-Shop and M.Video.'
- 第0轮 [S1阶段漏掉暖场和空间给予]：The salesperson jumped directly into product details without building rapport or giving the customer space to express needs, which may have made the initial interaction less comfortable.
  > Playbook coverage shows S1 missed 'space_giving' and 'warmup_greeting' actions.

### 销售经验（可写入培训手册）
1. Always lead with the lower-priced variant when the customer mentions a budget range, to avoid triggering price objections.
2. Use live demonstrations to overcome skepticism about compatibility or performance issues.
3. In S1, include a warm greeting and give the customer space to express their needs before diving into product details.
4. When quoting prices, immediately anchor to the customer's budget and highlight value equivalence (same battery, screen).

### 更优话术模板
- **Price is too high (55,990 ₽ exceeds budget).**
  > I understand. The good news is the 8GB+256GB model is only {price} ₽, which fits your budget of {budget} ₽. It has the same {key_features} as the higher model. Let me show you on the demo unit how smoothly it runs.
- **Does it support Google Play?**
  > Let me show you right here on the demo unit. We have GBox installed, and you can sign in to your Google account and use Play Store, Gmail, and YouTube just like on any Android tablet. Would you like to try it?
- **I'm worried about battery life.**
  > This model has a 10,000mAh battery, which easily lasts a full campus day. Let me show you the battery settings and estimated usage time on the demo unit. For your usage of 2h gaming and 3h notes, you'll have plenty of charge left.

### 可复用策略模式
- **现场演示破信任法**
  - 使用条件：When the customer expresses doubt about a feature (e.g., compatibility, performance).
  - 示例：Customer: 'Does it support Google Play?' Salesperson: 'Let me show you on the demo unit. Here's GBox, you can try it yourself.'
- **价格锚定换算法**
  - 使用条件：When the customer objects to the price of a high-end model.
  - 示例：Customer: '55k is too much.' Salesperson: 'The 8GB+256GB model is only 48,999 ₽, within your budget, with the same battery and screen.'
- **需求匹配降维法**
  - 使用条件：When the customer's needs are moderate and a lower-spec model suffices.
  - 示例：Customer: 'I need it for gaming and notes.' Salesperson: 'For your use, 8GB RAM is plenty. The 12GB model is overkill.'
