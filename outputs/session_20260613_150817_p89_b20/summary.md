### 销售经验（可写入培训手册）
1. When a customer demands a specific test, perform it immediately without unnecessary confirmations. Use the 'test-first, confirm-later' approach.
2. Avoid placeholder text or scripted phrases that don't make sense. Always prepare real data or be ready to improvise with accurate information.
3. For skeptical customers, use the 'prove it' technique: let the customer see the product perform under their exact conditions.
4. If the customer is impatient, minimize confirmations and focus on action. One confirmation is enough; multiple confirmations risk losing the customer.
5. In a remote demo, ensure all technical terms and numbers are accurate and relevant to the customer's context.

### 可复用策略模式
- **Test-First Confirmation**
  - 使用条件：When a customer asks for a specific performance test, especially if they are impatient or skeptical.
  - 示例：Customer: 'Does it lag with 20 layers?' Salesperson: 'Let me show you right now. I'll open 20 layers and switch between them. Watch the screen.'
- **Technical Anchor**
  - 使用条件：When a customer compares with a competitor (e.g., Procreate) and highlights a weakness.
  - 示例：Customer: 'Procreate pixelates at high zoom.' Salesperson: 'That's because Procreate uses raster sampling. Our device uses vector-linked texture maps, so the grain stays sharp at any zoom. Let me demonstrate.'
- **Impatience Deflection**
  - 使用条件：When a customer shows impatience with confirmations or questions.
  - 示例：Customer: 'Stop confirming and just do it.' Salesperson: 'You're right. Let me switch layers now. Watch the screen.'
