### 销售经验（可写入培训手册）
1. Always use in-store demonstration: When a customer asks 'show me how', physically demonstrate on a live device instead of describing steps. This builds trust and reduces skepticism.
2. Use 3B responses for objections: When a customer expresses doubt (e.g., 'InshAllah, this will really stop it?'), first empathize ('I understand your concern'), then provide evidence (e.g., show a video or testimonial), then state the benefit.
3. Confirm understanding with visual feedback: After guiding a step, ask the customer to describe what they see on the screen to ensure they are following correctly.
4. Address all concerns before moving on: The customer had a lingering question about password locking; the salesperson should have asked 'Do you have any other questions?' before proceeding to the overview.

### 可复用策略模式
- **Live Demonstration Trust Builder**
  - 使用条件：When a customer asks 'show me how' or expresses doubt about a feature.
  - 示例：Instead of describing steps, say 'Let me show you on this tablet' and physically perform the action while the customer watches.
- **3B Objection Handling**
  - 使用条件：When a customer uses phrases like 'InshAllah' or 'Are you sure?' indicating doubt.
  - 示例：Empathize ('I understand your concern'), provide evidence ('Here's a video of another parent using it'), then state benefit ('So your daughter can focus on studying').
- **Visual Confirmation Loop**
  - 使用条件：After guiding a step, to ensure the customer is following correctly.
  - 示例：Ask 'What do you see on your screen now?' and wait for their description before proceeding.
