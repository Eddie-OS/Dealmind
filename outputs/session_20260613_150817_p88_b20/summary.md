### 销售经验（可写入培训手册）
1. 1. When a customer asks about product stability or performance, always offer a live demo immediately—do not rely on verbal specs alone.
2. 2. Use price anchoring with urgency (e.g., 'today-only bundle') to overcome budget constraints, but ensure the price is within the customer's stated range.
3. 3. For high-skepticism customers, follow the 3B objection-handling sequence: empathize, reframe with evidence, and then ask for commitment. Skipping steps prolongs hesitation.
4. 4. In a retail setting, leverage physical stock and immediate availability to create a sense of instant gratification.
5. 5. When a customer asks for a demonstration, let them perform the action themselves (e.g., 'type a few lines') to build ownership and trust.
