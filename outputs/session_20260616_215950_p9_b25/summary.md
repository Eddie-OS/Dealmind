### 销售经验（可写入培训手册）
1. Never use placeholders like '准确价格我让同事现场给您调单核一下' when a customer asks for a specific number. If you don't know, say 'I need to check with my colleague' and then actually provide the number in the next turn.
2. In in-store sales, leverage physical demonstrations: hand the customer the tablet, let them type on the keyboard, and show the M-Pencil. This builds trust and reduces price focus.
3. When comparing with competitors, always use itemized, concrete numbers. Avoid approximations like '~RpX'—use exact figures.
4. If you make a mistake (e.g., using a placeholder), apologize and correct immediately. Do not repeat the same error.
5. For trade-in objections, have a pre-calculated worst-case scenario ready. Use a simple table or printed sheet to show the customer instantly.

### 可复用策略模式
- **Worst-Case Anchoring**
  - 使用条件：When customer demands trade-in value and is skeptical about low offers.
  - 示例：State the absolute worst-case trade-in (e.g., Rp20,000) upfront, then immediately show the net price and compare with competitor's best-case. This builds trust and shifts focus to total savings.
- **Bundle Value Stacking**
  - 使用条件：When customer compares bundle price with competitor's itemized costs.
  - 示例：List all included items (tablet, keyboard, pen) with their individual prices, then show the bundle discount. For iPad, add up separate costs to highlight the savings.
- **In-Store Demo Redirect**
  - 使用条件：When customer gets stuck on price or objections.
  - 示例：Physically hand the product to the customer and say, 'Here, try it yourself. While you do, let me calculate the exact numbers for you.' This breaks the objection loop and builds tactile trust.
