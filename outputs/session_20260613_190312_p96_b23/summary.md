### 销售经验（可写入培训手册）
1. When a customer explicitly asks to try a product, hand it over immediately—do not ask more questions.
2. Use the in-store environment for live demonstrations (tilt screen under light, play audio, show settings) to address concerns visually.
3. Always be prepared to close: know stock availability, pricing, and alternatives before the customer asks.
4. Avoid introducing uncertainty (e.g., 'not on sale in Italy') without offering a solution like a similar model or pre-order.
5. For family buyers, emphasize how the product benefits each family member (learning for kids, entertainment for all).

### 可复用策略模式
- **Immediate Hands-On Handover**
  - 使用条件：When a customer explicitly asks to try a product or shows strong interest in a feature.
  - 示例：Customer: 'Let me try the pen.' Salesperson: 'Here you go—write anything and feel the response.'
- **Live Environment Demonstration**
  - 使用条件：When the customer raises a concern that can be visually proven (glare, sound, speed).
  - 示例：Customer: 'Does the screen reduce glare?' Salesperson: 'Watch—I'll tilt it under the light. See? No mirror effect.'
- **Family Benefit Framing**
  - 使用条件：When the customer mentions family members or multiple use cases.
  - 示例：Customer: 'For the whole family.' Salesperson: 'This tablet works for Matteo's schoolwork, your Netflix nights, and even Sofia's drawing.'
