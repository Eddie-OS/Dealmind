### 销售经验（可写入培训手册）
1. Always have key certifications (SGS, TÜV) printed or saved on a demo device for immediate display. Never say 'I'll email it later' when the customer asks to see it now.
2. When a customer mentions a competitor's certification, be ready with a side-by-side comparison on a tablet or printed sheet. Use the store environment to show the difference physically.
3. For customers with low decision power, create urgency by offering a time-limited store incentive (e.g., 'if you decide today, I can include a free case') to encourage immediate purchase or at least a faster follow-up.
4. Use live demos that directly address the customer's specific use case (e.g., anti-glare for window seating) to make the benefit tangible.
5. Prepare a 'certification binder' or digital folder on the store tablet containing PDFs of all relevant certificates, warranty terms, and test reports for instant access.

### 可复用策略模式
- **Live Certification Showcase**
  - 使用条件：When a customer asks for proof of certification or feature claims.
  - 示例：Keep PDFs of all certifications on a demo tablet. When asked, immediately open the PDF and highlight key test results, then demonstrate the feature (e.g., anti-glare tilt) to connect the certificate to real-world benefit.
- **Competitor Side-by-Side Demo**
  - 使用条件：When a customer mentions a competitor's feature or certification.
  - 示例：Have a competitor device (e.g., Xiaomi) available in-store. When customer says 'Xiaomi has TÜV', pull out both tablets, show the certification comparison on a screen, and then do a live demo (e.g., tilt both toward light) to visually prove the difference.
- **Joint Decision Follow-Up Kit**
  - 使用条件：When a customer says they need to consult a spouse or partner.
  - 示例：Prepare a physical or digital 'decision kit' with a comparison chart, key benefits summary, and a special offer valid for 24 hours. Hand it to the customer and say, 'This has everything you and your husband need to decide. If you come back today, I can include this free accessory.'
