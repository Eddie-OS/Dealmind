### 销售经验（可写入培训手册）
1. Always confirm the customer's core concerns before diving into demos; use open-ended questions to uncover all objections.
2. For skeptical customers, prioritize live demonstrations over verbal claims; use tools like IR guns and FPS overlays to provide irrefutable proof.
3. After addressing performance concerns, proactively discuss price, warranty, and after-sales to prevent last-minute objections.
4. In-store demos should be interactive; let the customer see and touch the results themselves to build trust.
5. Avoid premature closing; ensure all potential objections are surfaced and addressed before asking for the order.

### 更优话术模板
- **How do I know the 144Hz panel doesn't ghost?**
  > Let me show you right here on the device. I'll open a fast-scrolling test and you can see for yourself — no ghosting, instant response. You can even try it with your own finger.
- **I'm worried about battery life during gaming.**
  > I understand. Let's run a 30-minute Genshin loop and measure the battery drain. You'll see the percentage drop and the FPS stays stable. We can also compare with other tablets if you like.
- **The price seems high compared to competitors.**
  > Let's compare the specs side-by-side. I'll pull up the competitor's model on our display and we can check the performance, display, and included accessories. You'll see the value in the complete package.

### 可复用策略模式
- **Live Proof Demo**
  - 使用条件：When customer expresses skepticism about performance metrics (temperature, FPS, latency).
  - 示例：Use an IR gun to measure temperature after a game loop, show FPS overlay at low battery, or run a touch latency test.
- **Interactive Verification**
  - 使用条件：When customer wants to verify a claim themselves.
  - 示例：Let the customer perform the test (e.g., swipe fast to check ghosting) or watch the measurement process.
- **Concern Confirmation Loop**
  - 使用条件：At the start of the interaction, before any demo.
  - 示例：Ask: 'What are your top concerns? Let me list them: battery, display, price? Correct?' Then address each one systematically.
