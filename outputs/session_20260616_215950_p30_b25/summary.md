### 销售经验（可写入培训手册）
1. Always provide concrete pricing and terms immediately; avoid placeholders like 'let me check with a colleague' unless you actually need to verify. If you need to check, say 'Let me confirm the exact price for you' and use a tool or ask a colleague in real time.
2. In-store, never send a customer to a website for warranty details. Have a printed warranty summary or show it on a tablet in the store. This keeps the customer engaged and prevents them from leaving.
3. Use a confirmation loop (reflection_paraphrase) to lock in pain points before discussing solutions. This builds trust and ensures the customer feels heard.
4. Localize all offers: mention specific local retailers (Liverpool, Palacio de Hierro) and local warranty coverage (Mexico City). This increases relevance and trust.
5. Avoid repeating the same placeholder phrase; it damages credibility. If you don't have the answer, say 'Let me get that information for you right now.'

### 可复用策略模式
- **Pain-Point Confirmation Loop**
  - 使用条件：When customer has expressed multiple pain points early in the conversation.
  - 示例：Salesperson: 'Just to confirm, the three reasons you're switching are X, Y, and Z, correct?' Customer confirms, then salesperson presents solution.
- **Local Retailer Anchoring**
  - 使用条件：When discussing pricing or financing options in a specific market.
  - 示例：Salesperson: 'At Liverpool, you can get 13 months interest-free on this model. Let me calculate your monthly payment.'
- **In-Store Demo for Objection Handling**
  - 使用条件：When customer raises a feature objection (e.g., glare, weight).
